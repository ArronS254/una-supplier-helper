Add this to Outlook

Download the add-in file, then in Outlook go to Get Add-ins, My add-ins, Custom add-ins, Add from file, and choose it. Your IT team can also give it to everyone from the Microsoft 365 admin centre.

Version 1.5

How to use it
  1. Enter your initials and your company’s email domain once. They’re remembered on this computer.
  2. Drag the email out of Outlook onto your desktop or a folder, then drag that file onto the email box. The note is written straight away. You can also click “Open email file”, or paste the email and click “Write note”.
  3. Check the note. If it’s wrong, tap the right update type or edit the text.
  4. For a tricky email, click “Ask Copilot”. It copies the email with instructions. Click the Copilot button on Outlook’s toolbar and paste. Paste, press Enter, then copy Copilot’s answer back into the box and click “Use Copilot’s answer”.
  5. Click the big navy copy button and paste into OrderWise. Click it again for the next action, then again for the date. The highlighted box shows what it will copy next.
  6. When you’re done, click “Reset” (or press Esc) to clear everything for the next email. Your initials and domain stay saved.
